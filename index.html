About

What this is

A free, searchable directory of UPSC Civil Services Mains topper answer copies. Search across every question inside the copies and jump straight to the page where a topper answered it.

About me

Hey, I am Hashin Jithu! I cleared the Civil Services Examination in 2021 with an All India Rank of 553. I built the Toppers Copy website as a small way of giving back to the aspirant community. You can find more of my writing at blog.hashin.me.

Credit & sources

This is a community effort, non-commercial and open source. The answer copies are published by coaching institutes and by the toppers themselves — they own them. This site only indexes them and links out, so aspirants have one place to search instead of a dozen.

The GS & Essay question-level index — which topper answered what, on which page — builds on open community work, including upsckata.com's Topper Copies. Optional-subject and newer copies are compiled from institute pages (Level Up IAS, Vision IAS, IMS4Maths, SuccessClap, LotusArise and others) and from what people send in. If anyone whose work is indexed here wants a change, open an issue.

Where the PDFs live

Every "Open PDF" / "Open copy" link points to a file hosted elsewhere — the institute or compiler that published it (ForumIAS, Vision IAS, NextIAS, Lukmaan IAS, GS SCORE, Rau's IAS, Level Up IAS, UnlockIAS, Sleepy Classes, Vishnu IAS…) or the topper's own Google Drive. No answer copy is stored on this domain. Rights holders who want a link removed can file an issue.

Contributing & moderation

  • Use Submit to add a missing copy, an optional-subject copy, or a topper's AIR / marks.
  • Every submission becomes a GitHub issue. A moderator checks the copy is genuine and adds an approved label; it's then applied to the site automatically within a couple of minutes.
  • Moderators are repo collaborators — see MODERATORS.md.
  • Code and data: github.com/hashin/topperscopy — PRs welcome.

Full static index

Prefer no JavaScript, or crawling the data? The complete static index of every topper and copy lists all rankers, their papers and links to each source PDF. Machine-readable data: copies.json, toppers.json, questions.csv, llms.txt.

Complete dataset

A consolidated backup of everything collected — every question, every topper's AIR and marks, including all accepted submissions — lives in the repo under dataset/ and is downloadable directly: questions.csv (flat table), toppers.csv, dataset.json (everything nested), manifest.json (checksums).
